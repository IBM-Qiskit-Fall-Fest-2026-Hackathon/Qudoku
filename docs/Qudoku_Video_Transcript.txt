QUDOKU — 3-MINUTE DEMO & PITCH VIDEO TRANSCRIPT
IBM Qiskit Fall Fest 2026 | Quantum for Humanity | Track 3: Education
Target Length: ~3:00 minutes (~435 spoken words at ~145 WPM)

========================================================================
0:00 - 0:30 | HOOK & PROBLEM (~70 words)
========================================================================
[Screen: Open Slide 1 or Qudoku landing view in Dark Mode on the 4x4 board]

Most beginners hit a wall when learning quantum computing—not because the ideas
are impossible, but because textbooks start with abstract Dirac notation, Bloch
spheres, and English-only tools before showing what entanglement actually does.

Meet Qudoku—an interactive quantum Sudoku learning platform built for Track 3:
Education. Everyone already understands Sudoku rules. Qudoku turns those
familiar row, column, and sub-box constraints directly into live Qiskit
SamplerV2 circuits.


========================================================================
0:30 - 1:05 | ACCESSIBLE BOARD & SUDOKU FOUNDATIONS (~85 words)
========================================================================
[Screen: Click a cell on the board, toggle Light/Dark Mode, hover an InfoTooltip icon, and briefly open the Board Size selector (4x4 to 16x16 Hex)]

Instead of forcing beginners into a 9x9 wall of numbers right away, Qudoku
supports five grid sizes—4x4, 6x6, 9x9, 12x12, and 16x16 Hex—across 22 curated
tutorials and sandboxes, plus custom uploads validated in milliseconds by a
Strict Mode Exact Cover solver.

We designed the UI to feel welcoming from second one: you get Sudoku.com-style
row, column, and sub-box highlights, matching-symbol highlights on filled cells,
a Light and Dark Mode toggle, and beginner hover tooltips on every control.


========================================================================
1:05 - 1:55 | LIVE QUANTUM CIRCUITS, GATE LAB & MITIGATION (~125 words)
========================================================================
[Screen: Select Tutorial 2 (Bell Pairs) on 4x4, highlight the glowing Cyan Bell tether, show the Qiskit diagram & histogram, toggle Gate Lab + X(q0) and M^-1 mitigation, then click Measure & Collapse]

Now watch how Sudoku constraints become quantum physics. Any empty cell holds
multiple legal candidates at once—a superposition you can inspect with Inspect
|psi>. When two cells share a bivalue pair, Qudoku detects a Bell pair—or you
can manually select cells to synthesize Bell, GHZ, W-state, or custom wildcard
ket circuits in Qiskit 2.6.

Running SamplerV2 simulates shots non-destructively so learners can experiment
safely: cyan bars show valid solutions, while rose bars reveal constraint
violations when you inject IBM Heron or Eagle noise, or intentionally break
entanglement in the Gate Lab. Turning on M^-1 readout error mitigation lifts
constraint fidelity back toward 98 to 100 percent—and clicking Measure and
Collapse collapses the circuit onto the board.


========================================================================
1:55 - 2:30 | CLASSICAL BASELINE & HONEST SCALING (~80 words)
========================================================================
[Screen: Switch briefly to the Cached Hardware Benchmarks tab or Slide 6 scaling table]

We also keep the science honest. Our classical Exact Cover solver checks 4x4
through 16x16 puzzles in 0.16 to 5.62 milliseconds. Today's noisy processors
cannot beat that yet—so Qudoku uses classical propagation for instant
validation, while teaching why quantum matters at scale: generalized N by N
Sudoku is NP-complete, quantum registers compress N symbols into ceil(log2 N)
qubits, and Grover search cuts unstructured branching quadratically from O(K)
to O(sqrt(K)).


========================================================================
2:30 - 3:00 | 8-LANGUAGE EDUCATION & CLOSING (~75 words)
========================================================================
[Screen: Switch language live (e.g., Hindi, Tamil, or Marathi), open the full-page Lessons (6 modules) and Quiz (8 questions)]

Finally, quantum education shouldn't depend on knowing English. Qudoku includes
full-page 6-module beginner lessons and an 8-question interactive quiz
completely localized across 8 languages: English, Hindi, Gujarati, Marathi,
Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, and Bengali.

Tested across 22 unique boards, backed by FastAPI and Qiskit Aer, and ready for
any classroom—Qudoku turns logic puzzles into real quantum intuition. Thank you!
